# System Reference Document 5.1

The core RPG rules in `crates/rpg` follow the System Reference Document 5.1,
adapted for a real-time game.

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1
("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at
<https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document>. The SRD 5.1 is
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
available at <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode>.

The material used is the rules' mechanics (stat modifiers, hit dice,
proficiency, attack rolls against armour class, damage dice and critical
hits), not its text. The rest of the RPG rules (attack speed, mana, the
experience curve, points per level, abilities) are our own.
